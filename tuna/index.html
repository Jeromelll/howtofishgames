Tuna: bait, fight and Albatross setup

Tuna is part of the fourth-island combat route. The community bait table assigns it Professional Boss Lure, distinct from the ordinary Professional Lure.

Prepare before reeling it in

Steam players report a difficult solo fight. One approach is to stage healing food near buildings, keep distance and use measured sniper shots. Do not assume stairs or doorways are safe: the same report describes Tuna reaching those positions.

Keep the defeated Tuna

The next encounter is Albatross, which approaches the Tuna. Keep the body until you are ready for that fight, rather than consuming your setup item.

Use the Island 4 preparation page to plan the two encounters together. Return to the object index for other catches.
