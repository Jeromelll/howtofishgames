Island 5: Volcano

Volcano is a common community name for the fifth numbered stop. The v1.1.3 table says the hazard-suit scientist exchanges five items for Fish Bucket, and lists beer, snail or any fish as hand-ins. The v1.05 route recommends upgrading the SMG and preparing cooked fish. Confirm the current prompt; these are community reports.

Before the final route

  • Check the scientist’s item counter before the exchange.
  • The v1.1.3 table lists Scientific Lure here.
  • Read the Bowhead Whale guide first.

The v1.05 guide describes this as its final island route; ordinary fish follow bait, not water. Previous: Island 4. Reviewed 26 October 2026; community route, not our playtest. Check current prompts before hand-ins.
