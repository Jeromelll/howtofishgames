How to Fish: how to get euros

A community-reported way to earn euros is to catch fish with Crab Carcass and sell them. The October 4–8 Steam discussion describes this route without requiring a successful casino bet.

The fishing and selling route

  1. Find the small sandy mini islands and collect Crab Carcass.
  2. Use the carcass as bait. Check the catch's sale currency before treating it as euro income.
  3. Cook suitable catches before selling if the displayed value improves. One player reports a 1.5× cooked value; that multiplier is community advice, not an official price table.
  4. Sell the catches and check the euro balance before returning for more bait.

Players report revisiting the bait island during longer fishing trips. The sources do not establish a timed respawn rule, guaranteed island order or euros-per-hour rate. Plan around what you actually find rather than a promised farming timer.

What should you spend euros on?

Snake's guide identifies the Wishbone as a euro upgrade that improves the chance of Drip catches. The official 1.1.2 patch adjusted its pricing. Check the current offer rather than relying on an old price list; we have not verified a maximum bonus.

If you are exploring equipment, the bow guide explains the merchant landmark and the limits of available upgrade information. As a planning suggestion, choose the purchase you want before spending the balance on optional activities.

Do you need to gamble?

No successful bet is part of the fishing-and-selling method above. The discussion also contains large gambling-win anecdotes, but those are not dependable earnings forecasts. A sale gives you the displayed proceeds; a bet should not be counted as guaranteed upgrade money.

What about Manta?

Players in the same discussion mention Manta when discussing euros, but we have not verified a fixed payout. Start with the Manta landmark notes if you want to investigate that encounter; do not budget around an unverified reward.

Before travelling again, check your radar. For catches and progression items, use the searchable guide index.
