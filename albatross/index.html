Albatross: Island 4 and the Tuna setup

Albatross follows the Tuna encounter on the fourth main island. Preserve the defeated Tuna; players describe the bird approaching to take it.

Set up before the bird arrives

Choose a fighting position before putting the body down. A Steam player recommends cover beneath the gift-shop bridge and ranged shots between attacks. Treat that as a player strategy: it does not guarantee protection in every version.

Plan both fights together

  • Finish Tuna first and keep its body.
  • Check ammunition and recovery supplies before triggering another encounter.
  • After combat, inspect the reward and finish the local quest dialogue before sailing.
