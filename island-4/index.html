Island 4: Rocks

Rocks is one community name for the fourth numbered island; labels vary, so follow the stage number and map marker. The v1.05 route links two encounters: bring up Tuna, then use it to draw Albatross. The guide recommends fishing for cash and upgrading weapons first; this is player advice, not a required build.

Check both encounters

  • The v1.1.3 table lists Professional Lure here.
  • Keep Tuna for the later encounter.
  • Review Albatross preparation before the second fight.

Ordinary fish follow bait, not water, according to the bait table. Check rewards and prompts before sailing. Previous: Island 3; next: Island 5. Reviewed 26 October 2026; community routes, not our playtest.
