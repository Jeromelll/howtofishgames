Island 1: Lighthouse

Community guides often call the starting area Lighthouse Island; treat labels as player terminology. In a v1.05 solo route, clams fund a rod, dagger and beer bottle. Check the sailor’s quest prompt and current shop before spending; that loadout is one speedrun option.

Before sailing onward

  • Check the sailor’s request and current hand-in prompt.
  • Prepare for Spider Crab; the speedrun route attacks after its charge.
  • Check the reward and radar before sailing.

The v1.1.3 bait table lists Hot Dog here and says ordinary catches follow bait, not water. Source review: 26 October 2026; community route, not our playtest. Next: Island 2.
