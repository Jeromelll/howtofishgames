Pufferfish: location, bait and boss fight

The Pufferfish quest is on the third main island. Speak to the swimmer wearing yellow floaties: he wants an endangered creature before giving you Carrot Bait.

Get Carrot Bait

Fish with Standard Lure, inspect your catch with the default F key, and offer a fish marked endangered to the swimmer. Bowlfish and Needlefish are relevant catches. Equip the carrot reward to summon the boss.

Prepare for the fight

PC Gamer's player recommends an upgraded SMG, moving continuously and using trees to break the pursuit. Avoid the purple poison. This is a reported strategy, not a required weapon or guaranteed terrain exploit.

Route context: main islands. Previous boss: Giant Piranha.
