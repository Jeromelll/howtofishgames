## Typeface

`im-fell-english.ttf` is IM Fell English Roman by Igino Marini, copied from the [official Google Fonts repository](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/imfellenglish) (`IMFeENrm28P.ttf`) and renamed for this project. The [Google Fonts metadata](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/imfellenglish/METADATA.pb) identifies the family, designer, and SIL Open Font License. The accompanying [`OFL.txt`](OFL.txt) is the license distributed in that same directory.

The typeface is an approximate stylistic choice for irregular serif headings; it is not represented as the game's original font.
