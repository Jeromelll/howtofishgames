Island 3: Beach

Beach is a common community label for the third numbered island. The local Pufferfish guide identifies a swimmer in yellow floaties who requests an endangered catch and gives Carrot Bait. The v1.05 route buys Standard Bait and watches for that quest fish. Check the catch label and NPC prompt before handing it in.

Prepare for the next step

  • Confirm the tourist’s endangered-fish request.
  • Use Standard Lure; keep the quest reward separate.
  • Review Pufferfish preparation before the encounter.

The v1.1.3 table lists Standard Lure here and says ordinary fish follow bait, not water. Previous: Island 2; next: Island 4. Reviewed 26 October 2026; community route, not our playtest.
