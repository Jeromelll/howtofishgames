How to play How to Fish

This is the Steam game How to Fish (Dazed Games), not a real-world fishing lesson. The loop is short. The usual confuse is thinking more catches unlock the next island.

Core loop

  1. Equip a rod and cast near water.
  2. When a fish bites, manage reel tension. Panic reeling snaps the line. Practice this on the starter shore — snapped lines are cheaper here than on Island 2.
  3. Sell catches for cash. Money buys rod, line, bait, boat, and weapons.
  4. Spend first on rod / line, then boat, then a basic weapon. Later islands mix fishing with shooting.
  5. Clear the island quest and hand in the boss drop. That is what writes the next Radar marker.

What this loop does not do

Selling more fish makes you richer. It does not skip the unlock chain. If the next destination is missing, check the local quest and reward hand-in before farming more catches. Order: Lighthouse → Forest → Beach → Rocks → Volcano.

Radar

  • The boat + Radar are how you leave the starter loop.
  • Check that you have your Radar before leaving an island; use the numbered main route to identify your next quest.

Co-op

In 2–4 player sessions, split roles: one steadies the boat, others fish or cover combat when wildlife turns aggressive. The Giant Piranha is easier if one person melee-clears the small piranhas while another shoots the boss.
