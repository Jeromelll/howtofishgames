Where are you stuck?

  • I sold a lot of fish and the next island is still locked
  • I killed the Giant Piranha and Island 3 never opened

Island chain (cannot skip)

  1. Starter / lighthouse

    Cast–sell, gear, boat, Radar. First hour.

  2. Forest

    3 leeches → Modified Leech → Giant Piranha skeleton → Island 3.

  3. Desert → rocks → volcano

    Same rule every time: kill, pick the drop, hand it in. Full order.
